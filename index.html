Corporate Profile

Leading Through
Technical Excellence.

Established in 2020 and based in Akasia, Pretoria, Encori Trading and Projects is a premier 100% Black female-owned construction firm. We bridge the gap between architectural concept and structural reality by managing the entire project lifecycle with clinical precision.

Our firm specializes in a Turnkey Integrated Model. This means we handle your project from the very first line of drafting, through rigorous municipal approval processes, to bulk earthworks, reticulation, and the final site handover. By consolidating these services, we offer our clients a single point of accountability, reduced overhead costs, and a significantly accelerated project timeline.
